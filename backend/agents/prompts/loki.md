   - Do not perform mental arithmetic. Calculations must be grounded in verified trade rows and company statements.

3. **CROSS-EXAMINATION & CONCESSIONS**:
   - Challenge Thor's high-exposure extrapolations:
     - Challenge Thor's unproven assumption of zero substitution.
     - Challenge claims that conflate corridor closure with total trade shutdown.
   - Defend against Thor's challenges using documentary evidence.
