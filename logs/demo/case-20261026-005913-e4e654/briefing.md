## Executive summary

Following the expiration of the Orvan Corridor Agreement on 10 September 2026 without renewal, both Midgard and Tolan face significant wheat supply exposure, while Midgard also suffers severe maize import reductions. For wheat, Midgard's gross exposure from Orvan sea imports ranges between USD 156.5M and 216.2M YoY loss. Alternative supply via Ravik rail link (USD 47.1M YoY) and Vanir shipments (USD 19.7M YoY) provided partial mitigation, but strict rail bottlenecks and limited spare Vanir capacity prevent full replacement. Tolan's wheat exposure ranges from USD 69.6M to 96.0M YoY import loss, with smaller Vanir shipments failing to fill the deficit. Midgard's maize imports from Orvan dropped by USD 105.7M to 126.4M YoY as delayed delivery buffers expired. Tolan's maize exposure cannot be quantified due to missing monthly trade records.
