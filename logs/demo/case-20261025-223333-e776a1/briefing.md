## Executive summary

The expiration of the Orvan corridor agreement on 10 September 2026 has resulted in a substantial contraction of grain trade value for both Midgard and Tolan. In the fourth quarter of 2026, wheat import values from Orvan fell by approximately USD 156.5M for Midgard and USD 69.6M for Tolan compared to 2025, representing a drop of over 50% in primary sea route value. Midgard's maize imports showed temporary resilience due to delayed delivery terms but eventually collapsed to a USD 126.4M quarterly loss. While alternative supplies from Vanir and rail routes through Ravik have partially mitigated these losses, they are described as smaller, more expensive, and insufficient to bridge the total trade gap.
