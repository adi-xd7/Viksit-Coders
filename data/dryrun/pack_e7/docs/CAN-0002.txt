LNG carriers turn south as Ymir Canal shuts

[P1] Brightwave Shipping said on Thursday that six of its LNG carriers bound for Midgard and Ketra had turned south to sail around the cape.

[P2] The detour adds about nine days to a one-way voyage from Solvar, the company said, and some March cargoes will arrive in April.

[P3] Solen Energy said its liquefaction plants in Solvar keep running at normal rates.
